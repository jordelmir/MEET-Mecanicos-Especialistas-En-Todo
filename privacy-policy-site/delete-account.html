Google Play User Data Policy Compliance

Request Account & Data Deletion

Application: OBD2 Elysium Vanguard (MEET) — Package: com.elysium369.meet

Developer: Elysium Vanguard / MEET Diagnostics

In accordance with Google Play's User Data Policy and global data protection regulations (GDPR, CCPA, LGPD), users have the right to request permanent deletion of their account and associated personal data.

How to Delete Your Account In-App

If you have the MEET application installed on your Android device:

  1. Open the MEET app.
  2. Go to Ajustes (Settings) from the navigation menu.
  3. Scroll to the Cuenta (Account) section.
  4. Tap "ELIMINAR CUENTA" and confirm the confirmation dialog.

This triggers the server-side deletion request, revokes local provisioning credentials, and wipes all cached data on the device immediately.

Scope of Deleted Data

Upon processing an account deletion request, the following data categories are permanently purged:

  • Authentication & Identity: Account profile, email address, password hashes, and OAuth links.
  • Vehicle Records: Assigned vehicles, VIN associations, and personal maintenance logs.
  • Cloud Diagnostics: Diagnostic session history, uploaded live sensor telemetry, and workshop profiles.
  • Communications: Push-to-Talk room participation logs and chat messages.

Data Retention Exceptions (Mandatory Regulatory & Security Requirements)

Certain records may be retained as permitted or required by applicable laws and regulations:

  • Forensic Certified PDF Inspection Hashes: Cryptographic SHA-256 hashes of previously issued pre/post inspection certificates are preserved in immutable audit logs to protect third-party vehicle buyers against odometer fraud, safety tampering, and counterfeit inspection claims.
  • Financial & Tax Records: In-app purchase transaction receipts and mobility payment settlement records are retained for the statutory period required by fiscal and tax authorities.
