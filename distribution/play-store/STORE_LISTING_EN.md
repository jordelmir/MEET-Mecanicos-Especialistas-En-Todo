### Full Description (Google Play Formatted)

MEET (Mechanics Specialists In Everything) is the all-in-one automotive engineering platform connecting professional OBD-II diagnostics, certified forensic inspection reports, OEM technical parts compatibility, and on-demand roadside assistance.

PROFESSIONAL OBD-II / EOBD AUTOMOTIVE DIAGNOSTICS
• Compatible with standard ELM327 Bluetooth and WiFi diagnostic adapters.
• Read and clear Diagnostic Trouble Codes (DTCs): Powertrain (P), Chassis (C), Body (B), and Network (U).
• Real-time Live Sensor Data: RPM, Engine Coolant Temp (ECT), Short/Long Term Fuel Trims (STFT/LTFT), Fuel Rail Pressure (FRP), Mass Air Flow (MAF), timing advance, and O2 sensors.
• Freeze Frame analysis to capture the exact telemetry state when a fault occurred.
• I/M Readiness monitors for smog and emissions inspection compliance.

CERTIFIED FORENSIC INSPECTION REPORTS WITH SHA-256 & QR
• Comprehensive pre-purchase inspections and vehicle peritaje reports for used car evaluation.
• Pre-Scan and Post-Scan proof reports documenting defect resolution with physical evidence.
• Canonical cryptographic SHA-256 integrity seal and tamper-evident QR code independently verifiable by inspectors, insurers, and car owners.
• Immutable maintenance and repair history tied to the vehicle VIN.

TECHNICAL PARTS MARKETPLACE & ENGINE COMPATIBILITY
• Match and verify replacement auto parts based on active DTCs, OEM part numbers, and vehicle engine specifications.
• Detailed 3D mechanical diagrams and exploded sub-assembly views.
• Anti-fraud labor and parts repair estimates for transparent workshop billing.

VANGUARD ROADSIDE ASSISTANCE & WORKSHOP NETWORK
• On-demand roadside assistance and tow truck dispatch with live map tracking.
• Push-to-Talk (PTT) high-clarity voice communication with certified automotive specialists.
• High-performance vector map interface powered by MapLibre with dynamic navigation routing.

ENGINEERING CONSOLE & DIGITAL VEHICLE ACCESS
• Built-in terminal and diagnostic utilities for deep CAN-bus analysis and protocol testing.
• Dual UX: Guided mode for vehicle owners, Dense expert mode for workshop master technicians.
• Vehicle digital key pairing via NFC Host Card Emulation (HCE).

PRIVACY, SECURITY & INTEGRITY FIRST
• Local-First: Diagnostic telemetry and vehicle data stay under your control on your local device.
• Strong encryption in transit using standard HTTPS and TLS protocols for cloud synchronization and media sessions.
• Transparent data lifecycle with an instant in-app account and data deletion path.
