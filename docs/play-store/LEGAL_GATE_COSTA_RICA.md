## 1. Google Play Approval ≠ Legal Authorization

Google Play Store approval means the application complies with Google's distribution policies. **It does NOT constitute authorization to operate a paid transportation service in Costa Rica.** Separate legal and regulatory compliance is required.

---

## 2. Costa Rica Regulatory Framework

### 2.1 Transporte Remunerado
- **Ley de Transporte Público Terrestre (Ley N° 7966)**: Regulates public land transportation.
- **Reglamento para el Servicio de Taxi (Decreto Ejecutivo N° 38906-MOPT)**: Regulates taxi services.
- **Ride-hailing apps** operate in a legal gray area in Costa Rica. No specific law authorizes or prohibits platforms like Uber, DiDi, or inDrive.
- **Current status**: Uber operates in Costa Rica without explicit authorization but with tacit government tolerance.

### 2.2 Key Legal Considerations
- MEET should be positioned as a **technology platform connecting users**, not as a transportation company.
- Drivers operate as **independent contractors**, not as MEET employees.
- MEET does **not** own vehicles or employ drivers.
- Insurance: Each driver must carry their own auto insurance (SOA — Seguro Obligatorio de Automóviles).

### 2.3 Financial Regulations
- **SINPE (Sistema Nacional de Pagos Electrónicos)**: Costa Rica's national electronic payment system.
- Wallet topups via SINPE are permitted but must comply with:
  - **Ley de Fortalecimiento de la Competencia (Ley N° 9637)**: Antitrust compliance.
  - **Regulación de Servicios de Pago (Decree N° 40680-MECON)**: Payment service regulations.
- MEET does **not** act as a financial intermediary. It facilitates connections; payments are between parties.

### 2.4 Tax Obligations
- **Dirección General de Tributación (DGT)**: All income must be reported.
- **Comprobante Electrónico (TICE)**: Electronic invoicing required for all transactions.
- MEET must issue electronic invoices for platform commissions.
- Drivers are responsible for their own tax obligations as independent contractors.

### 2.5 Data Protection
- **Ley de Protección de la Persona frente al Tratamiento de sus Datos Personales (Ley N° 8968)**: Costa Rica's data protection law.
- Requirements:
  - Informed consent for data collection.
  - Right to access, rectify, and delete personal data.
  - Data minimization principle.
  - Security measures for data protection.
- MEET's privacy policy must comply with Ley 8968 in addition to Google Play requirements.

## 3. MEET's Legal Positioning

### 3.1 Platform Model
MEET is a **technology platform** that:
- Connects passengers with independent driver-contractors.
- Provides vehicle diagnostic tools and fleet management software.
- Facilitates electronic payments via SINPE.
- Does **not** own, operate, or control vehicles.
- Does **not** employ drivers.
- Does **not** set fares (fares are set by the platform algorithm based on market conditions).

### 3.2 Terms of Service
MEET's Terms of Service must clearly state:
- MEET is a technology platform, not a transportation company.
- Drivers are independent contractors.
- MEET facilitates connections but does not guarantee availability.
- Users assume responsibility for their own actions.
- Insurance is the driver's responsibility.

### 3.3 Driver Agreement
Each driver must agree to:
- Maintain valid auto insurance (SOA).
- Maintain a valid driver's license.
- Operate a vehicle that meets minimum safety standards.
- Comply with all applicable traffic laws.
- Report accidents and incidents promptly.
