# Third-party Safety data

Status: proposed policy; legal review required. Report only material relevant to
a legitimate safety purpose. Do not upload unnecessary identity documents,
contact details, banking records, intimate material or unrelated bystanders.
Collecting media does not itself establish permission to redistribute it.

Original media and exact location remain private. Public source ingestion stores
canonical URL, provider identity, content hash and immutable revision; ingestion
does not grant a right to reproduce full articles or publish allegations.
Provider activation requires provenance, permitted-use review and an explicit
server configuration. The Repretel adapter contract is provider-neutral; no
agreement or actual Repretel dataset is claimed by synthetic tests.

Review rectification, counterclaims and takedown requests with source provenance.
Changing external content creates a revision and reevaluation, never a silent
overwrite or automatic public case. Do not infer guilt from correlation or
media coverage. Protected financial data has no public-app ingestion adapter.
