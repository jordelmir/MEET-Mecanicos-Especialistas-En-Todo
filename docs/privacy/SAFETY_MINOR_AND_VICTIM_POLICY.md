# Minors and victims

Status: proposed safeguarding procedure; specialist/legal approval pending.
Avoid identifiers, precise whereabouts, school/home information and unnecessary
media involving minors, victims or witnesses. Keep originals private and limit
review to explicitly authorized staff. Do not publish images of victims/minors
or sensitive narratives by automatic ingestion or aggregate calculations.

Review public summaries for indirect identification, including titles, dates,
source URLs, exact event descriptions and small groups. A coarse geographic
area alone is not a safeguarding clearance. Use delayed/suppressed aggregates
and independent reviewer/publisher decisions; escalate uncertainty for human
review. Missing institutional records never imply inaction.

Record safeguarding decisions, rectification and withdrawal privately. Enable
only contact/reporting channels the operator has actually staffed and approved;
do not imply the app replaces emergency response, protective services or courts.
